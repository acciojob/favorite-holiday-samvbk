Goa


Goa is a beautiful holiday destination in India, famous for its beaches, scenic views, Portuguese architecture, nightlife, and delicious food.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach - Famous for its beautiful beach and water activities.
  2. Fort Aguada - A historic Portuguese fort with amazing views.
  3. Basilica of Bom Jesus - A famous historic church in Old Goa.
  4. Dudhsagar Falls - A spectacular waterfall surrounded by greenery.
  5. Calangute Beach - One of Goa's most popular beaches.

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry - A traditional and flavorful seafood dish.
  • Prawn Balchão - Spicy prawns prepared with a tangy sauce.
  • Pork Vindaloo - A spicy and tangy Goan specialty.
  • Bebinca - A traditional layered Goan dessert.
  • Chicken Cafreal - Chicken cooked with flavorful green spices.
